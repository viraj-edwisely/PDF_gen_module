# Conventions for ME, PI, XE and AE

- Units: SI throughout; stresses in MPa (N/mm²), forces in kN, moments in kN·m, pressures in kPa or MPa (gauge unless
  "absolute" is stated); temperatures in K inside thermodynamic formulas (°C only for differences).
- Thermodynamics: heat INTO the system positive, work done BY the system positive: Q − W = ΔU (+ ΔKE + ΔPE). Specific
  quantities lower-case (v, u, h, s per kg). Air as an ideal gas with R = 0.287 kJ/kg·K, c_p = 1.005, c_v = 0.718,
  γ = 1.4 unless the question gives other values. Steam and refrigerant properties: the values the question gives.
- Strength of materials: tension positive; sagging bending moment positive; shear force positive when the left part
  is pushed up (state it in the section). Second moment of area I about the axis of bending.
- Fluid mechanics: ρ for density, γ = ρg for specific weight; depths measured vertically down from the free surface;
  g = 9.81 m/s² unless given; buoyancy acts through the centroid of the displaced volume.
- Theory of machines and vibrations: angles in radians inside formulas; natural frequency ω_n in rad/s and f_n in Hz
  stated separately.
- Manufacturing and industrial engineering: the formula variant the question names (Taylor's tool life, EOQ, ...).
- GATE gives: material properties (E, G, ν), fluid properties, gas constants and table values it expects to be used.
- Typical traps: gauge vs absolute pressure, °C in an ideal-gas formula, the sign of work in compression, mass vs weight,
  the axis about which I is taken (rolling vs pitching, the dimension cubed), vertical depth vs distance along an inclined
  plate, using an end-point pressure for a varying-pressure process, radians vs degrees.
