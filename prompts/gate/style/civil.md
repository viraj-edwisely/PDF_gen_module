# Conventions for CE and related papers

- Units: SI; stresses in MPa (N/mm²), loads in kN, moments in kN·m, unit weights in kN/m³, pressures in kPa.
- Structures: tension positive; sagging moment positive; reactions upward positive; state the sign convention of a slope
  or deflection formula before using it. Determinate before indeterminate: say which method applies.
- Geotechnical: γ (bulk), γ_sat, γ' = γ_sat − γ_w with γ_w = 9.81 kN/m³ unless given; effective stress σ' = σ − u;
  void ratio e, porosity n, water content w, degree of saturation S with G_s w = S e.
- Fluid mechanics and hydrology: depths measured vertically down; Manning's n, Chezy's C as given; discharge in m³/s.
- Surveying: whole-circle bearings clockwise from north (0° to 360°); latitude = L cos θ, departure = L sin θ with signs
  by quadrant (N and E positive).
- Transportation and environmental: the formula variant and the code edition the question names.
- Indian codes (IS 456, IS 800, IRC): use only the values the question or the book gives; never quote a clause number from memory.
- Typical traps: bearing vs angle from the x axis, effective vs total stress, gauge pressure, units of k (m/s vs cm/s),
  the dimension cubed in I, the sign of the departure in the second and third quadrants.
