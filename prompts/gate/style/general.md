# Conventions for other papers (CH, BT, XL, MA, ST, GA and the rest)

- Units: SI, with the units the paper's previous questions use (mol, kmol, kJ/mol, mg/L, ...); state them with every value.
- Notation: the standard textbook notation of the subject, defined once in the notation sheet and used everywhere.
- Mathematics and statistics: vectors and matrices named once; probability P(A), E[X], Var(X); logarithms natural unless stated.
- General Aptitude: exact wording of the given data; no outside knowledge; units and rounding stated in the question.
- GATE gives: physical constants and table values it expects to be used; never assume a value it does not give.
- Typical traps: units and their conversions, rounding of intermediate values, the base of a logarithm, misreading
  "at least" / "at most", and assumptions the question states but students skip.
